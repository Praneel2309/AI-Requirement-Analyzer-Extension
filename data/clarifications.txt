1. Relevance should be determined by how closely a candidate's technical skills, work experience, and projects match the required and preferred skills in the job description.

2. Skills should have the highest importance, followed by relevant experience and then relevant projects.

3. Good companies should not directly increase a candidate's score. Company reputation may only be used as contextual information and must not dominate ranking.

4. Project strength should be evaluated using relevance to the job, technical complexity, demonstrated impact, and evidence of implementation.

5. Years of experience should be considered as one factor but should not override strong evidence of relevant skills and projects. A highly relevant fresher may rank above an experienced candidate with weaker relevance.

6. Historical hiring data can be used, but it must first be cleaned, structured, and checked for historical bias before being used for model training.

7. The system should achieve at least 90% agreement with a human review benchmark on a representative validation dataset.

8. Every candidate ranking should include an explanation showing the major factors contributing to the score.

9. Gender, college name, and other protected or proxy attributes should not directly influence the ranking. Fairness should be evaluated across relevant demographic groups.

10. Resume processing should normally complete within 3 seconds per resume.

11. The system should support batch processing for large numbers of resumes.

12. The MVP should be delivered within 8 weeks.
